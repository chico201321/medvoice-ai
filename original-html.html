AI Technology Implementation

Real-Time Speech Processing

Implementing streaming speech recognition with medical vocabulary adaptation. Using WebRTC for real-time audio capture and processing with low latency.

Technologies: OpenAI Whisper API, WebRTC, custom medical corpus

Medical Entity Recognition

Advanced NLP for extracting medical entities (symptoms, diseases, medications) from unstructured conversation text with 95%+ accuracy.

Technologies: spaCy, BERT, custom medical NER models

Diagnostic Intelligence

AI system trained on medical protocols and case databases to suggest diagnoses, recommend tests, and flag potential issues.

Technologies: Fine-tuned GPT models, medical knowledge graphs, ICD-10 integration

Anomaly Detection

Machine learning algorithms to detect unusual prescription patterns, potential fraud, and deviations from medical protocols.

Technologies: Isolation Forest, clustering algorithms, rule-based systems
